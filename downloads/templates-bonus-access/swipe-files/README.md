# ClickCoach JV Swipe Files

Use these swipes as starting points for your audience. Replace every placeholder before sending or posting.

## Placeholders

- `[PARTNER NAME]` - JV partner name or brand
- `[AFFILIATE LINK]` - your ClickCoach affiliate link
- `[WEBINAR LINK]` - your webinar registration link
- `[DATE]` - webinar date
- `[TIME]` - webinar time and timezone
- `[BONUS]` - optional partner bonus
- `[AUDIENCE]` - your audience name, community, list, or niche

## Files

- `01-webinar-announcement-and-followup-emails.md` - 4-email webinar sequence
- `02-email-sales-funnel-emails.md` - 4-email sales sequence
- `03-social-media-posts-with-graphics.md` - 10 social posts with graphic references
- `graphics/` - 10 SVG graphics sized 1080x1080

## Notes

These are written in a high-energy direct-response style, but they are intentionally editable. The strongest results usually come from adding one personal story, one audience-specific pain point, and one clear reason you recommend ClickCoach.
