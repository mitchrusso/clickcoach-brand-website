Business operations

Run the practice without letting the practice run you.

ClickCoach connects the commercial and administrative work around coaching: prospects, client records, money, messages, documents, and reports. One update moves through the system instead of being copied into five tools.

Replace the Patchwork

From inquiry to client

Know who needs attention and where they came from.

The built-in CRM keeps prospects alongside the practice they may join. Track follow-up dates, outreach history, sources, and conversion without maintaining a separate sales database.

  • Prospects pipelineFollow-ups, message history, and a direct convert-to-client flow.
  • Source attributionTag referrals, webinars, ads, and custom sources, then report on them.
  • Client roster and profilesSearch active and archived clients with their complete coaching history.
  • CSV importBring clients and prospects in bulk, assign groups, and send onboarding packages.
  • Groups and lifecycleOrganize cohorts and archive inactive clients without losing the record.
