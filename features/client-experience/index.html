Client experience

Give clients one place to keep becoming who they came to become.

The client portal carries the work between sessions. Assignments, answers, progress, courses, files, invoices, and next steps stay organized around the relationship instead of scattered across inboxes.

A portal worth returning to

The next action is never buried in an old email.

Clients sign into a secure, branded portal designed for phones and desktops. They can see what is due, respond to questions, enter progress, learn, pay, and return to the work without asking you to resend a link.

  • Branded mobile-first portalLogo, colors, themes, navigation options, and installable PWA behavior.
  • Homework and session viewAssignments, related content, and accountability questions in context.
  • Quick-access buttonsGive clients one-tap access to Zoom, scheduling, resources, or custom destinations.
  • Prospect accessOffer a limited portal experience before a prospect becomes a client.

Visible transformation

Clients can see change before they have to remember it.

Define up to ten meaningful metrics for your practice. Clients enter values from the portal, while charts and history give both of you a shared view of progress.

  • Custom stats, colors, ranges, and client settings
  • Goals and progress entry from mobile
  • Trend charts inside the coaching workflow
  • Monthly cross-client reporting
  • Evidence for reviews, renewals, and testimonials

A complete journey
