Keep the brand coherent while each coach remains credible.

Coach profiles can include bios, tags, and testimonials. Companies may enable an optional public directory, while partner and white-label environments can use their own presentation and URLs.

  • Rich coach profilesGive every coach space for a bio, specialties, tags, and testimonials.

SlipMeter for teams

Combine the work behind the coaching into one management view.

Invite team members to use SlipMeter without giving them full ClickCoach access. Each person records time, client, activity, notes, outcomes, billing status, progress, energy, and follow-up, then submits those slips securely to the organization.

  • Combine submitted slips across multiple SlipMeter users
  • Store team slips in the ClickCoach cloud account
  • Review and approve slips before they become official
  • Run management, utilization, and invoice-ready reports
  • Bring approved work logs into the related client record
