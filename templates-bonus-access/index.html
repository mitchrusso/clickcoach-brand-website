How to use them

Customize first, then publish.

These templates are written to give you a strong starting point. The best results come when you add a personal story, a reason you recommend ClickCoach, and a clear call to action.

  1. Download the ZIP or open one template file from the list above.
  2. Replace the placeholders such as partner name, affiliate link, webinar link, date, time, bonus, and audience.
  3. Add your own voice by adding a short story, a specific client problem, or why your audience should care.
  4. Use the graphics with the social posts, or upload them into your email and webinar promotion tools.
  5. Test every link before sending, especially your affiliate link and webinar registration link.

Placeholders to replace

[PARTNER NAME] Your name or brand [AFFILIATE LINK] Your ClickCoach affiliate link [WEBINAR LINK] Your webinar registration link [DATE] Webinar date [TIME] Webinar time and timezone [BONUS] Optional partner bonus [AUDIENCE] Your list, group, niche, or community
